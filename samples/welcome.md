Use **Ctrl/Cmd+F** to open find. Try searching for `Lampage`, `copy`, or `unique-token-alpha`.

## Selection

Select any of this paragraph in **read** or **edit** mode, then press Ctrl/Cmd+C
or use the floating **复制** button.

Repeat phrase for multi-match: find-me find-me find-me
